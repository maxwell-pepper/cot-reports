COT Reports

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

In short: COT Reports does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking.

What the extension does

COT Reports is a Chrome extension that displays publicly available Commitments of Traders (COT) data published by the U.S. Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC). When you open the extension, it downloads the latest report data and shows it to you. Everything is processed inside your browser.

Information we collect

None. The extension does not collect, record or transmit any personal information, browsing history, website content, keystrokes, location, financial details or any other data about you. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and it has no user accounts.

Network requests the extension makes

To work, the extension contacts two third-party services. Like any website you visit, these services receive standard technical information that browsers send with every request, such as your IP address and browser type. The extension does not add any personal information to these requests.

  • CFTC Public Reporting Environment (publicreporting.cftc.gov), to download the public COT report data. See the CFTC privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com), to load the typeface used in the extension. See the Google privacy policy and the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

Permissions

The extension requests access only to publicreporting.cftc.gov, so it can download COT report data. It does not request access to the websites you visit, your tabs or your browsing activity.

Data sharing and sale

Because the extension does not collect user data, there is no data to sell, share or transfer to anyone.

Children's privacy

The extension does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If a future version of the extension ever collects data, this policy will be updated before that version is released.
